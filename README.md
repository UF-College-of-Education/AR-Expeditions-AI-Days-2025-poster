# Advancing Educational Innovation Through AI-Enhanced Design and Immersive AR Environments

![AR Expeditions Poster](https://github.com/UF-College-of-Education/AR-Expeditions-AI-Days-2025-poster/blob/main/AI-Days-Poster_AR-Expeditions.png)

**Authors:** Kayla Sharp, Macy Geiger, Rachel West, Jay Rosen, Juan Marin  
**Conference:** UF AI Days, October 24–29, 2025

## Abstract
This project explores the use of artificial intelligence (AI) throughout the full development cycle of an augmented reality (AR) learning tool designed to engage students in grades 3–5 and their families. Developed in collaboration with the Lastinger Center for Learning at the University of Florida, the project integrates interactive story-based AR experiences to promote meaningful family engagement and spark curiosity in shared learning.

Our poster highlights how AI tools supported each stage of development: from ideation and brainstorming, to content creation, technical implementation, and the AR gameplay and user experience. Within each phase, we present key challenges and the AI-enhanced solutions that enabled the team to accelerate creativity, improve collaboration, and optimize production. Tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, GitHub Copilot, AR Foundation, Eleven Labs, and Unity ML Agents were used to streamline workflows, enhance accessibility, and elevate design quality.

By demonstrating how AI shaped the creative and technical processes of developing an AR-based family learning experience, this project showcases the transformative potential of AI-assisted design in educational technology and its ability to connect learners and families through interactive storytelling.
